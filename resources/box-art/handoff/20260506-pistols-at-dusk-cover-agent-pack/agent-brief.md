# Agent Brief: Fresh Pistols at Dusk Cover

This is the canonical brief for this handoff pack. If it conflicts with `chatgpt-web-brief.md` or `submitted-prompts.md`, trust this file.

## Goal

Create a completely fresh painted cover image for **Pistols at Dusk** using only the source files in this folder.

This should be treated as a new generation from source, not as an edit or continuation of any previously generated output from this thread.

## Source Priority

Use these files first:

- `pistols-a-devils-choice-reference.png`
  For the bartender's demonic menace, silhouette, goblet/cauldron energy, and supernatural feel.
- `bartender-at-bar-reference.png`
  For the bartender's recognisable human identity, face, hair, stern expression, clothing, and tavern context.
- `tavern-entrance-background-reference.png`
  For the Fool & Flintlock visual language, warm dusk lighting, and Pistols world architecture.
- `Ultima5box.jpg`
  For the classic confrontation staging, portrait cover proportions, and old-school 1980s fantasy box-art composition.
- `crossed-pistols-logo-reference.png`
  For the crossed-pistols emblem.
- `underware-logo-black-bg.png`
  For the small publisher mark in the bottom-right.
- `chomsky.otf`
  For the title treatment `Pistols at Dusk`.

Use these as supporting style/layout references:

- `pistols-dawn-style-reference.png`
- `pistols-dawn-scene-reference.png`

Do not assume any other support files exist outside this folder.

## Core Image

Paint a retro fantasy game-box cover for **Pistols at Dusk**.

The scene is a confrontation in Act III, deep in the underworld. A lone human duelist faces the bartender transformed into a demonic host. The image should feel like a classic staged fantasy cover: dramatic, readable from a distance, heroic, ominous, and painterly.

## Composition

- Portrait cover layout, roughly classic box-art proportions.
- Strong foreground duelist and looming opposing figure.
- The duelist stands in the foreground in a confident three-quarter rear view.
- His left hand hangs low holding a lantern.
- His right arm is raised and aimed outward with an antiquated flintlock pistol.
- Coat tails, boots, and stance should feel swashbuckling and human.
- The bartender-demon occupies the opposing side of the composition and feels taller, darker, and more supernatural.
- The bartender-demon should still read as the bartender from `bartender-at-bar-reference.png`, not as a generic demon.
- His expression and bearing should feel like a host welcoming someone to their doom.
- A second flintlock in the bartender-demon's hand is good.
- A goblet, cup, or vessel in the other hand is also good if it helps preserve the bartender identity.

## Identity Of The Bartender-Demon

The transformed figure should clearly inherit from the bartender in `bartender-at-bar-reference.png`:

- same face shape and stern age
- same hairline / hair impression
- same innkeeper / bartender social presence
- echoes of his shirt, vest, apron, or bar-host silhouette

The demon version should feel like the bartender revealed, not replaced.

## Style

Blend:

- classic 1980s fantasy box-art staging
- the cover-composition instincts of `Ultima5box.jpg`
- the warmer hand-painted Pistols-at-Dawn look
- the moody underworld menace from `pistols-a-devils-choice-reference.png`

The result should be:

- painterly
- graphic
- readable
- bold in silhouette
- warm in its lighting
- not overly noisy or micro-detailed

Aim for simplified planes, controlled texture, and a strong poster-like read rather than grimdark digital over-rendering.

## Colour Direction

- warm lantern amber and old gold as the key lights
- deep dusk oranges and ember highlights
- dark plum-black, soot-black, and muted teal in the shadows
- restrained supernatural violet accents only where they help the bartender feel uncanny

The overall balance should feel more like warm painted cover art than neon horror.

## Typography And Branding

Please include these in the final composition:

- title at the top: `Pistols at Dusk`
- use `chomsky.otf` if the workflow supports real compositing
- otherwise emulate that blackletter/newspaper Gothic feel closely
- `crossed-pistols-logo-reference.png` may appear as a secondary emblem, but it should not overpower the title
- `underware-logo-black-bg.png` should appear subtly in the bottom-right corner as a publisher mark

## Preferred Workflow

One-pass final generation is preferred if the tool genuinely supports a fresh source-driven cover image.

If the tool is better with compositing, use this fallback:

1. generate the cover painting from the scene references only
2. composite the title with `chomsky.otf`
3. place `crossed-pistols-logo-reference.png` and `underware-logo-black-bg.png` afterwards

## What To Avoid

- do not use any previously generated cover output from this thread as a visual reference
- do not drift into generic grimdark digital fantasy rendering
- do not let the branding overwhelm the painting
- do not lose the bartender's recognisable identity
