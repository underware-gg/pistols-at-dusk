# v0.8.0 — 2026-06-01

**Make canonical TileGenesis the sole tile provenance record**

v0.8.0 lands ADR 0005's canonical genesis acceptance criteria: a frozen
`TileGenesis` record becomes the single runtime-owned provenance for every
tile, the loose per-record provenance fields are removed, and the scene-runtime
export emits per-stamp genesis. The breaking data-contract change to
`TileRecord` is what drives the MINOR bump. Two off-theme changes ride along on
contiguity: an ADR-0006 deferral record and a standalone reference-matcher
guard.

- A new frozen `TileGenesis` dataclass is the sole runtime-owned provenance for
  each tile, carrying `kind` (`sheet`/`synthetic`), the sheet cell,
  `source_group`, `cluster_ids`, and synthetic-derivation facts (`derivation`,
  `parent_construction_ids`, `parent_tile_ids`, `authored_notes`); one-hop
  lookups are reachable on both the unit and the registry.
  - Important identifiers: `scripts/tile_library.py` (`TileGenesis`, `TileLibraryUnit.genesis_for`, `TileLibraryUnit.genesis_for_alias`, `TileLibraryRegistry.genesis_for`, `TileLibraryRegistry.genesis_for_alias`), `docs/architecture/decisions/0005-runtime-tile-genesis-and-provenance.md`
- **Breaking:** `TileRecord.genesis` is now a required, non-optional field and
  the loose provenance fields `sheet_col`, `sheet_row`, `cluster_ids`, and
  `source_group` are removed from `TileRecord`; every reader now routes through
  `tile.genesis`, including the catalog resolver, the sheet-cell index, the
  source-region lookup, and the tile query model.
  - Important identifiers: `scripts/tile_library.py` (`TileRecord`, `_resolved_family_tile`), `scripts/tile_families.py` (`_resolve_tiles_from_catalog`, `_index_tiles_by_sheet_cell`, `_tile_query_value`, `TILE_GENESIS_QUERY_FIELDS`), `tests/test_tile_families.py`, `tests/test_scene_expansion.py`
- The scene-runtime export now emits each placed entity tile's canonical
  genesis payload, and synthetic construction-cell tiles derive their
  `parent_construction_ids` from the composing construction rather than merely
  lacking a sheet cell.
  - Important identifiers: `scripts/harness.py` (`_tile_genesis_payload`, `_entity_instance_payload`, `export_layout_scene_runtime`), `scripts/source_ingest_ops.py` (`build_semantic_catalog_entries`, `inspect_source_cell`), `tests/test_harness.py`, `docs/functional/README.md`, `docs/architecture/README.md`
- ADR-0006 records the decision to defer cross-kit interchangeability,
  reframing the construction-system "cross-kit constructions" open question as
  a decided deferral pending machine-verifiable seam profiles that would
  supersede the `connects_on` adjacency rule.
  - Important identifiers: `docs/architecture/decisions/0006-defer-cross-kit-interchangeability-to-seam-profiles.md`, `docs/architecture/decisions/README.md`, `docs/architecture/construction-system.md`
- The reference matcher now fails fast when two loaded sources resolve to the
  same `source_id`, closing a silent collision at the source-loading trust
  boundary (M5).
  - Important identifiers: `scripts/reference_tile_match.py` (`assert_unique_source_ids`, `load_source_tiles_from_specs`), `tests/test_reference_tile_match.py`
