# v0.4.0 — 2026-05-30

**Build the parametric_frame construction system end-to-end**

v0.4.0 adds `parametric_frame` as a third construction kind — the 2-D analogue
of `parametric_run` — wiring it through the construction loader, the entity
placement renderer, and the public inspection exports, then authoring the
border-kit and `gold_room` frame constructions and migrating the party_menu
frames onto them. The party_menu migration reshapes those frame scene ops from
the old `box`+`style` shape to `entity`+`construction`+`params`, a breaking
data-contract change for those instances.

- `parametric_frame` lands as a construction kind in `scripts/tile_families.py`:
  `ParametricFrameConstruction` plus the `FrameSlot` / `FrameCornerSlot` slot
  types join the `Construction` union, with `ParametricFrameConstructionConfig`
  parsing, `_build_parametric_frame_construction` resolution, and
  `_validate_parametric_frame_construction` enforcing at-least-one-corner and
  `min_width`/`min_height` >= 2. Corners resolve four ways — 1x1 by
  `corner_<key>` role, fat NxM via a `cells` grid, flip-derived, or borrowed by
  tile id — and edges carry `fill_mode`, flip-derivation, and role/tile
  overrides.
  - Important identifiers: `scripts/tile_families.py`, `ParametricFrameConstruction`, `FrameCornerSlot`, `FrameSlot`, `_build_parametric_frame_construction`, `_frame_corner_slot`, `_frame_slot`, `FRAME_FILL_MODES`, `EntityFootprintSpec`
- The harness renderer places frame entities end-to-end: corners stamp once at
  each anchor, present edges tile between corner extents, an optional fill tiles
  the interior, and flip-derived corners/edges emit flip-bearing stamp refs so a
  symmetric kit declares its art once. The `_placement_ref` helper is deduped to
  module level and shared across metatile, run, and frame placement.
  - Important identifiers: `scripts/minimal8_harness.py`, `_parametric_frame_tile_placements`, `EntityTilePlacement.stamp_ref`, `_placement_ref`, `_construction_tile_placements`, `_public_construction_entry`, `_construction_preview_image`
- The six border-kit constructions and the fat-corner `gold_room` kit are
  authored in `minimal8/constructions.json`, and their compose roles
  (`corner_*`, `edge_*`, `fill`) are bound across `minimal8/tiles.json`.
  - Important identifiers: `ui.frame.gold.gap`, `ui.frame.gold.smooth`, `ui.frame.gold.simple`, `ui.frame.less_ornate.gap`, `ui.frame.less_ornate.smooth`, `ui.frame.panel.smooth`, `ui.frame.gold_room`, `prototypes/minimal8-harness/tile-families/minimal8/constructions.json`, `prototypes/minimal8-harness/tile-families/minimal8/tiles.json`
- The party_menu frames migrate onto `parametric_frame` kits, reshaping their
  scene ops from `box`+`style` (`ui_outer_screen_reference` /
  `ui_inner_window_reference`) to `entity`+`construction`+`params` against the
  new `ui.frame.outer_screen` and `ui.frame.inner_window` constructions — a
  breaking change for those instances; the `box` op itself is unchanged.
  - Important identifiers: `prototypes/minimal8-harness/scene-templates/party_menu_character_stats.json`, `ui.frame.outer_screen`, `ui.frame.inner_window`
- Supporting data fixes correct the gold-frame border-kit cluster tag to
  `ui.column_4.cluster_01` and complete the `head_study` `spare_03` 2x2 cells,
  covered by dedicated tests.
  - Important identifiers: `prototypes/minimal8-harness/tile-families/minimal8/ingestion.json`, `prototypes/minimal8-characters/constructions.json`, `tests/test_tile_families.py::test_ui_gold_frame_collection_is_tagged_cluster_01`, `tests/test_tile_families.py::test_head_study_spare_03_construction_is_full_2x2`, `tests/test_tile_families.py::test_ui_border_kits_have_complete_compose_role_bindings`
- The frame system is covered by new placement and build tests, and inspect-family
  guide PNGs (`sheet_grid.png`, `source_layout*.png`) are gitignored so generated
  artefacts no longer land in family directories.
  - Important identifiers: `tests/test_scene_expansion.py::FrameOpExpandStampsTests`, `tests/test_tile_families.py::ParametricFrameConstructionBuildTests`, `.gitignore`
- ADR-0005 (Runtime Tile Genesis and Provenance) is recorded as a landed
  decision record describing the runtime-owned provenance model and noting its
  enforcement is still pending; the older runtime-tile-genesis note is repointed
  to the ADR and `parametric_frame` is marked implemented in the
  construction-system doc. This records the decision, not its implementation.
  - Important identifiers: `docs/architecture/decisions/0005-runtime-tile-genesis-and-provenance.md`, `docs/architecture/decisions/README.md`, `docs/architecture/runtime-tile-genesis-and-provenance.md`, `docs/architecture/construction-system.md`, `docs/architecture/README.md`
